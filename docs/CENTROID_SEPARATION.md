# §4.1 · Repair or replace, decided by measurement

Inter-archetype centroid distances across the stored Essential Mirror results, as instructed.
No code was changed to produce this. The verdict is in §4.

---

## 0. What the sample is, and what it is not

850 stored Essential Mirror results. **This is not a respondent sample**, and the numbers below
must not be read as one. Answer patterns divide sharply:

| distinct answer values in a session | results |
|---|---|
| 5 (varied) | 332 |
| 4 | 2 |
| 2 | 254 |
| 1 (every item the same) | 262 |

Over half are patterned or uniform — generated by test runs, not by people. Those inflate ties
mechanically: **on all 850, the gap between a reader's top two archetypes is exactly zero 54% of
the time.** That figure is an artefact of uniform answering and should be discarded.

Everything below uses the **334 varied-answer results** (≥ 4 distinct values). Even those are
mostly machine-generated with spread rather than human, so what this measures is the
**instrument's internal geometry — whether the six archetype item sets can separate profiles at
all** — not how a population distributes across them. That happens to be exactly what R3 asked,
which is why the question is answerable from what we hold.

## 2. Centroid distances

Each result is a six-dimensional vector of archetype percentages. Results are grouped by the
primary the instrument currently assigns; the centroid is the group mean; scatter is the mean
distance from a member to its own centroid. **Ratio = centroid distance ÷ mean scatter of the two
groups** — the figure that matters, because a large distance between two loose clouds is not
separation.

Within-group scatter: Rock 13.9 · Challenger 19.1 · Voyager 20.6 · Visionary 22.7 · Diplomat 24.0
· Empath 25.9

| pair | distance | ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Diplomat · Empath | 21.8 | **0.87** |
| Challenger · Visionary | 22.1 | 1.06 |
| Voyager · Empath | 25.5 | 1.10 |
| Voyager · Visionary | 26.6 | 1.23 |
| Empath · Visionary | 30.2 | 1.24 |
| Voyager · Challenger | 26.0 | 1.31 |
| Empath · Rock | 26.4 | 1.33 |
| Voyager · Diplomat | 30.9 | 1.39 |
| Challenger · Empath | 35.0 | 1.56 |
| Diplomat · Visionary | 36.7 | 1.57 |
| Challenger · Rock | 27.7 | 1.68 |
| Diplomat · Rock | 33.5 | 1.77 |
| Voyager · Rock | 31.3 | 1.82 |
| Challenger · Diplomat | 40.0 | 1.86 |
| Rock · Visionary | 36.9 | 2.01 |

**Mean ratio 1.45. Fourteen of fifteen pairs separate at 1.0 or better. One pair — Diplomat and
Empath — does not.**

## 3. Two supporting numbers

**Nearest centroid agrees with the current highest-score primary in 79% of cases.** So a region
model would keep roughly four readings in five exactly as they are, and change one in five — a
repair, not a different instrument wearing the same name.

**The top-two gap on varied answers: median 7 points; exactly tied 15%; within 3 points 29%.**
This is the number the tie rule has to answer, and it is the strongest argument for the region
approach independent of the centroids. The current code breaks a tie by whatever
`sorted(..., reverse=True)` returns first, which for equal scores is dictionary insertion order —
so at present a **15% share of readers are assigned an archetype by the order the six were typed
into a JSON file.** That is not a scoring dispute; it is arbitrary, and it is live.

## 4. Verdict

**Regions work. Repair the existing instrument; the Delta Mirror rewrite is not justified by
this evidence.**

Keep the hundred items, keep the Delta, and change only how the archetype is derived and shown:

1. **Nearest region by distance**, with the distances printed — which also makes the reading
   auditable by the person holding it.
2. **A tie rule that declines to rank** when the top two sit closer together than a stated
   margin, naming both instead. That closes R3 by construction and removes the insertion-order
   assignment.
3. **Diplomat and Empath are the pair that rule will fire on most**, at ratio 0.87. Two honest
   options, and this one is a decision rather than a measurement: either the tie rule simply
   names both, which is truthful and costs nothing, or the two item sets are reallocated to pull
   them apart — which is a scoring change and needs its own version.

The one number worth putting on the methodology page whichever way this went: on varied responses,
fourteen of the fifteen archetype pairs separate by more than the spread within each of them, and
the one that does not is Diplomat and Empath, which is why we decline to choose between those two
when a profile sits between them. That is a real statement about our own instrument's structure,
and it is the first one we have been able to make.

## 5. What this does not answer

- Whether the archetypes correspond to anything outside the instrument. Internal separability is
  not validity.
- How **people** distribute across the six. That needs real respondents, and the honest position
  until then is that we do not know.
- The 50-item lens overlap in `docs/ARCHETYPES_SPEC.md` §2 — Torchbearer/Visionary sharing eight
  of ten items with other archetypes, and items 7, 34 and 36 feeding no archetype at all. Region
  assignment does not fix that; it makes it visible, because a printed distance derived from
  overlapping item sets invites the question.
